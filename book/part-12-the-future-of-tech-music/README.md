# Part XII — The Future of Tech Music

Rules become algorithms, probabilities, generators, learned models, adaptive systems, and human–machine workflows. These chapters treat AI as software: capable, limited, data-dependent, testable in parts, and never a substitute for artistic judgment. Chapters 244–307 are the final numbered part; no Part XIII follows.
