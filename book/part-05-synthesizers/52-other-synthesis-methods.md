# Chapter 52 — Other Synthesis Methods

**Wavetable** synthesis reads/interpolates stored periodic shapes and may move among them. **Granular** synthesis arranges short sound grains. **Physical modeling** simulates selected behaviors of vibrating/exciting systems. **Sampling** maps recorded audio to events, often with pitch/time shaping. **Vector** synthesis moves among sound sources; **phase distortion** reshapes an oscillator's phase mapping; **hybrid** instruments combine families.

Each offers different control metaphors and useful territories, but none guarantees a genre or quality. These are orientation sketches, not implementation recipes or priority claims. Roads [29] provides the broader synthesis taxonomy; later DSP and programming parts supply machinery.
