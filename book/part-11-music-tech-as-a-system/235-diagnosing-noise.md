# Chapter 235 — Diagnosing Noise

> **Status:** reviewed educational model. Hardware behavior is not probed.

Noise may reflect weak wanted signal plus excessive later gain, environmental sound, a connection fault, software generation, or electromagnetic/grounding interaction. Labels such as “ground loop” are hypotheses, not diagnoses.

Mute or disconnect only safe, user-serviceable branches; substitute known-safe cables/sources; compare input, interface, and software meters; change one variable and log the result. Never defeat protective grounding, open mains equipment, or improvise power modifications.
