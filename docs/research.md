# Research and citation standard

Every substantive chapter ends with `## References`. Prefer standards/specifications, official documentation, primary technical material, established textbooks, peer-reviewed work, university material, then reputable secondary context. Inspect every cited source; never infer its contents from a title or search snippet. Record author/organization, title, edition/version, publisher, stable URL or identifier, and access date for mutable pages in the project bibliography and a source note.

Citations should sit beside the supported claim and point to the bibliography entry. Separate sourced fact, pedagogical simplification, author interpretation, and experimental result. Verify changing software behavior against the documented version. Do not use a citation merely because it is authoritative: it must support the claim.

For new source work, save an inspection note under `references/source-notes/` containing date, relevant sections/pages, claims supported, limitations, and the inspector's initials. Copyrighted material must be summarized rather than copied. The author's house-music book remains a named placeholder until its bibliographic details and contents are supplied.
