# Listening lab — Build an electronic track

## Objective
Connect audible changes to inspectable score and arrangement data.

## Conditions
Run `python -m tech_music.production`. Keep playback device and level fixed. Compare stages in numeric order, then compare the one-loop grid with the full arrangement.

## What to listen for
Record when pulse, backbeat, subdivision, low-register motion, harmony, motif, and texture become audible. Compare exact repetition with the build and main return.

## Observations
For every comparison, write (1) the data/layer difference and (2) the listening observation. Do not substitute “better” for evidence.

## Reflection
Which layer has a clear purpose? Which could be removed? Does the transition support your intended attention level?

## Limitations
The renderer, speakers, room, hearing, order effects, and preferences constrain conclusions. This is an educational A/B comparison, not a controlled perceptual study.
